## Who we are
Realtr provides multi-tenant websites for real estate agents. This policy covers data Realtr processes
for account holders (realtors/brokerages) and visitors to tenant sites.

## Data we collect
- **Account data:** name, email, organization, role.
- **Site content:** pages, media, and settings a tenant authors.
- **Listing data:** REALTOR.ca DDF data synced under the tenant's credentials (see the DDF terms).
- **Leads:** contact details visitors submit through tenant site forms (name, email, phone, message),
  with consent capture.
- **Billing:** handled by Stripe; Realtr stores only customer/subscription identifiers, never card data.
- **Operational:** logs and audit records of account and administrative activity.

## How we use it
Operate and secure the service, deliver leads to the tenant's CRM, process subscriptions, and meet
legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

## Sharing
Stripe (billing), the tenant's chosen CRM (e.g. Follow Up Boss), REALTOR.ca/CREA (listing data), and
infrastructure providers — each only as needed to provide the service.

## Access, export, and deletion
Account owners can export their organization's data and permanently delete their organization from
**Dashboard → Data & privacy** (see `../data-handling.md`). Visitors who submitted a lead may request
access or deletion via the tenant or Realtr support.

## Retention
Data is retained for the life of the subscription and until an owner-initiated erasure; see
`../data-handling.md`.
